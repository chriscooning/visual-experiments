The WebGPU ones need iOS 27 or later on iPhone and iPad. The rest run on anything, potatoes included.

Light & Glass · WebGPU

Prism Ring

Rings of tilted dichroic glass tiles on an ink-blue wall, turning slowly in opposite directions. Carry a torch round them with your pointer or finger, and the glass-tinted shadows swing round and glow out of the dark. Drag (two fingers on touch) to change the angle, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap for new colours.

WebGPU · Glass · Ray traced

Sliced Light

A glowing sphere built from a stack of flat frosted discs, caged in sheets of dichroic glass whose colour shifts as you turn it. Grab any point and drag to turn it, grab the rim to spin it like a wheel, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap to change the light, double-tap to reset.

WebGPU · Glass · Interactive

Poppy Hill

Poppies sway from their stems and close at night; mist fills the hollow at dawn. Drag the sun or moon anywhere in the sky, or under the horizon for night; the day turns on a timer or only on click.

Vineyard Night

A dripping blue sky with purple speckle and wandering trails of white dots over sage hills with oaks along the crests, a dotted vineyard, and luminous currents flowing through the dark brush, curling round eddies that drift and fade, all lit by a moon that drifts across the sky, or stays where it's put. Tap the sky or drag the moon to move it, or pull the trails.
